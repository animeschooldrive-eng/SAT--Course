Rule of 180

Angles on a straight line always add up to 180 degrees.

Key Notes & Concept Overview

The Rule of 180° is a foundational geometric concept frequently tested on the Digital SAT.

  • Straight Line Angles: Adjacent angles forming a straight line add up to 180°. (A + B = 180°).
  • Supplementary Angles: Two angles totaling 180°.
  • Triangles Interior Sum: Interior angles of a triangle total 180° (a + b + c = 180°).
